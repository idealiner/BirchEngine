# PrincessOwliviaCB v0.2.0

Gameplay and presentation update focused on cleaner pacing, clearer HUD visuals, and smoother web play.

## Highlights

- Simplified gameplay loop with one active enemy and one active butterfly at a time
- Randomized side entry for enemies and butterflies to keep encounters fresh
- Stronger per-level speed scaling while keeping logic simple
- Level-start grace protection to avoid instant collisions
- Butterfly HUD icon redesign with visible lower wings and pink/purple styling
- Improved text shadow tone for better depth and readability
